Manage subscription ↗

For refunds or a missing activation key, email support with your Paddle receipt reference.

Check these first.

  1. Workbooks should share a template. Corresponding worksheets and cells need to represent the same things.
  2. Review differences before export. Mixed text, headings or changed structures may need attention.
  3. Use supported input formats. Import .xlsx or common BIFF8 .xls workbooks. Export is always .xlsx.
  4. Check your license connection. Direct editions may need internet access for trial activation, paid activation and updates.
  5. After a subscription renewal, refresh activation. Re-enter your existing key in the desktop app to obtain the new signed license period.

Offline activation

If the computer running xlsOne cannot reach the activation service, open its license activation dialog and copy the device code displayed there. From a device with an internet connection, email support@xlsone.com with that device code, your purchase email and Paddle receipt or transaction reference.

Ask for help verifying your purchase and obtaining a signed offline license file for the direct desktop edition. Where supported by your release, import that file using the app’s offline activation option. Keep the file private; it is tied to your licensed device. No online signing tool is available on this page.

Offline activation does not require your spreadsheets. Do not send workbook contents, card numbers, passwords or raw hardware serial numbers. A renewed subscription may require an updated signed license file.

Keep your data private.

Do not email payment card details, passwords or unrelated personal data. We do not need your financial workbooks to answer a billing question.
