For refunds or a missing activation key, email support with your Paddle receipt reference.

Check these first.

  1. Workbooks should share a template. Corresponding worksheets and cells need to represent the same things.
  2. Review differences before export. Mixed text, headings or changed structures may need attention.
  3. Use supported input formats. Import .xlsx or common BIFF8 .xls workbooks. Export is always .xlsx.
  4. Check your license connection. Direct editions may need internet access for trial activation, paid activation and updates.
  5. After a subscription renewal, refresh activation. Re-enter your existing key in the desktop app to obtain the new signed license period.

Offline activation

If the computer running xlsOne cannot reach the activation service, use this page from a computer with an internet connection. Open xlsOne’s license activation dialog on the target computer and copy the device fingerprint displayed there.

A license for your specific device.

Creating a file activates the supplied device and may use one of your license’s device slots. Copy the fingerprint carefully. No spreadsheets or raw hardware serial numbers are needed.

Use the activation key you received after purchase. Keep it private.

Copy all 64 characters from the app’s license dialog on the computer you want to activate.

The key and device fingerprint are sent securely to the activation service when you submit. They are not placed in the page URL or saved in browser storage.

  1. Save the downloaded xlsone.license file.
  2. Transfer it to the computer with the matching device fingerprint.
  3. In xlsOne’s license dialog, choose the offline activation option and import the file.

Keep the file private. A renewed subscription may require a new signed file. If activation fails, email support@xlsone.com with your purchase email and Paddle receipt reference. Do not send card numbers, passwords or confidential workbooks.

Keep your data private.

Do not email payment card details, passwords or unrelated personal data. We do not need your financial workbooks to answer a billing question.
