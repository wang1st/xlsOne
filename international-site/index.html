A few good questions

Before your next report.

What kinds of spreadsheets work best?

xlsOne is designed for workbooks that use a matching template, with the same worksheet structure and corresponding cells. Check and align differences before relying on a consolidated result.

Are my Excel files uploaded?

No. Import, consolidation and export run on your computer. License activation and update checks connect to online services, but do not need your spreadsheet contents.

Can I check where a total came from?

Yes. Source tracing lets you inspect the original values behind a consolidated cell, so you can investigate differences before exporting.
