Refund policy

A fair start.
A clear way to ask.

Last updated: October 29, 2026. This policy applies to xlsOne purchases processed through Paddle.

Request a refund within 14 days

If xlsOne does not fit your needs, contact support@xlsone.com within 14 days of the charge to request a refund. Include the email used at checkout, your Paddle receipt or transaction reference, and a short explanation. Do not send full card details.

How requests are handled

We review the request and work with Paddle, the merchant of record, to process an eligible refund. You may also contact Paddle through the help information on your receipt. Processing time and the date funds reach your account depend on Paddle and your payment provider.

Cancellation is separate

Cancel future renewal in the customer portal at any time. Canceling stops the next renewal; it does not automatically refund a charge already made. If you want both, cancel renewal and send a refund request.

After a refund

A refunded or reversed purchase may have its related paid license access revoked. Keep your own files and exported results; they remain yours.

Your rights

This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights or remedies that apply to your purchase. If you have a concern outside the 14-day period, contact us so we can review the circumstances.
