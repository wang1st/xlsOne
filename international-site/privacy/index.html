Privacy policy

Your spreadsheets
stay yours.

Last updated: October 29, 2026. This policy covers the xlsOne international website and direct desktop editions.

Spreadsheet contents

xlsOne imports, processes and exports spreadsheets on your computer. We do not upload your workbook contents to our servers for consolidation. Files, results and local preferences remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

License activation and updates

Direct desktop editions connect to online services to activate trials and licenses or check for updates. Activation may send a license key, device name and a locally generated device identifier or hardware-component hashes. We use these to validate a license, enforce device limits and prevent repeated trial activation. These requests do not include spreadsheet contents.

Purchases and subscriptions

Paddle handles checkout, subscription billing, applicable taxes and payment-related records as merchant of record. Paddle may collect your email, billing address, payment details and transaction information. We receive the information needed to provide your license, maintain subscription access and answer support requests. We do not receive your full payment card details. Read Paddle’s privacy policy for its processing.

Purchase sessions

The checkout page uses browser session storage to keep a temporary purchase credential so you can retrieve your activation key after a payment or page reload. This is functional storage, not advertising tracking. Keep the purchase tab open until you have saved your key. Clearing browser data or closing the tab may remove this credential.

Website delivery and logs

Cloudflare delivers the website and online activation services. It may process request information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps and requested paths to deliver content, protect services and diagnose failures. We do not add advertising trackers, cross-site tracking scripts or analytics cookies to this website. Payment pages and the Paddle customer portal operate under Paddle’s own policies.

Support and retention

If you contact us, we use your email and the information you provide to respond. License and transaction records are kept as needed to provide access, resolve billing issues, prevent abuse and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Avoid sending confidential workbooks or unrelated personal information.

Your choices and requests

You can manage payment and subscription information in the customer portal. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information we hold, email support@xlsone.com. Some transaction information may need to be retained for legal or accounting reasons. We will review your request and explain any relevant limits.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product or services change. The date above identifies the current version. Contact support@xlsone.com with privacy questions.
