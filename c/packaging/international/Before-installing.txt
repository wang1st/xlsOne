xlsOne international desktop edition

Download the release that matches your operating system from https://xlsone.com/download/.
The installation package includes the software licenses and third-party notices.
On macOS, drag xlsOne.app to Applications and open it from there.
Open the application and choose Language > English if your system uses another language.
For a trial or paid activation, open the License dialog. After renewal, re-enter your existing key to refresh the local license.
